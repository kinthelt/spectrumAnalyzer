Strip setup

Bass end

Mirrors everything, for a strip mounted the other way round. Applies at once.

Each LED shows one frequency band, lowest at the bass end. About 150 LEDs suits today's ESP32-S3; more need more processing. Changing these restarts the lights.

Apple Home & Google Home

In Apple Home: + › Add Accessory, then scan the code. In Google Home: + › Device › Matter-enabled device. The apps warn that the lights aren't certified (they're home-made): choose to add them anyway.

Firmware update

In the Arduino IDE, choose Sketch › Export Compiled Binary, then pick SpectrumAnalyzer.ino.esp32s3.bin from the sketch folder. The strip turns dim blue while the file arrives, flashes green, and the lights restart; it takes about a minute.

Maintenance

Learn room noise

For when a new steady sound (a fan, an air purifier) makes the spectrum flicker. Turn music off first: the lights treat whatever they hear for 10 seconds as background.

Forget Wi-Fi

For a new router or password. The lights leave this network and open their own, Spectrum-Setup, for entering new Wi-Fi details. This page stops working until then.

Reset settings

Mode, colours, brightness and all presets go back to the defaults. Wi-Fi and the strip setup are kept.
